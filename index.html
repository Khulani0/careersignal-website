Why it's different

No gimmicks. Just what it actually does.

Nothing invented

CareerSignal never makes up a job, a date, or a skill you didn't mention. If something's unclear, it asks. It doesn't guess and fill in the blanks for you.

Real dates, properly ordered

Actual Month/Year dates for every job, laid out in the right order. No vague "6 months to a year" ranges to paper over gaps.

Built for who actually needs it

Matriculants. Retail and hospitality workers. Security officers. Domestic and care workers. Not just office professionals with a laptop and a LinkedIn.

Where you already are

Everything happens on WhatsApp, in the language you already write in. No new app to download, no account to create.
