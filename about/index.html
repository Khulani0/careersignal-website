What CareerSignal is

CareerSignal writes real CVs over WhatsApp. You tell it your story, in your own words, by typing, sending a voice note, or uploading a CV you already have. It never invents a job, a date, or a skill you didn't mention. If something's unclear, it asks rather than guesses. You see the result before you pay anything.

Who it's built for

The people usually priced out of CV help: matriculants writing their first CV, retail and hospitality workers, security officers, domestic and care workers. Not just office professionals with a laptop and a LinkedIn profile. Everything happens on WhatsApp, in the language you already write in.
