Terms of Service

Last updated: 4 September 2026

1. What CareerSignal is

CareerSignal is a document generation service. You send us your work history, by voice note or text, over WhatsApp. We generate a CV and, on Mid and Advanced, a cover letter. You review a preview before you pay. CareerSignal is not a job board, does not place you in a job, and never charges for access to job listings or referrals.

2. Pricing

CareerSignal charges one flat price per tier. There are no add-ons, no extra charges for revisions, and no separate fee for a human review. Everything you get is included in the price below.

TierPriceWhat's included
BasicR49One-page CV, choice of 6 templates, 1 AI fix, 1 Escalation to a real person
MidR99Up to two-page CV, choice of 10 templates, cover letter included, 2 AI fixes, 1 Escalation to a real person
AdvancedR199Up to three-page CV, choice of 16 templates (rolling out, see template count note below), cover letter, job-tailored keyword pass, 3 AI fixes, 2 Escalations to a real person

An "AI fix" is a revision round done by the bot from your feedback. An "Escalation" is a review or rewrite by a real person on our team, the same business day. Each tier includes a fixed number of both, at no extra cost. Once a document has used its included fixes and Escalations, that document is complete, no further revisions are available on it at any price. To keep building after that, start a new document.

3. Template availability

Basic offers 6 templates. Mid offers 10. Advanced offers all 16 in the WhatsApp conversation; this website's own template gallery is still rolling out the last 6, so you may see fewer shown here than the bot actually offers. We will not charge Advanced pricing for fewer templates than promised without saying so here first.

4. Preview before payment

You see your CV before you pay for it. If it's not right, you are not charged.

5. Escalation, human review

At every tier, you can ask for your CV to be reviewed by a real person on our team rather than only the AI, included in your tier price at no extra charge: 1 Escalation on Basic, 1 on Mid, 2 on Advanced. Escalation requests are handled by our team directly, not by an automated system, and are turned around the same business day.

6. What we never do

  • We never charge you to see job listings, sponsor information, or vacancy referrals. That information, where it exists, is free, permanently.
  • We never invent details on your CV. If something is unclear, we ask you rather than guessing.
  • We never promise a CV will get you a job, an interview, or a specific outcome. We produce a well-formatted, honest document.
  • We never share your login credentials or submit job applications on your behalf without your direct review and approval.

7. Your data

You can request deletion or export of everything CareerSignal holds about you at any point in the WhatsApp conversation, by typing what you want. Payment records are retained for 7 years as required under the Companies Act 2008. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

8. Refunds and disputes

Because you approve a preview before paying, refund requests are handled case by case through WhatsApp. Contact us directly through the same number you used to generate your document.

9. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as the product changes. The version in effect is always the one published on this page, dated at the top.
