Privacy Policy

How CareerSignal collects, uses, and protects the information you share with it.

Draft, pending legal review. The sections below describe how CareerSignal actually handles your information today. They have not yet been reviewed by a lawyer and should not be treated as final or binding until that review is complete.

What's collected

The career information you give CareerSignal over WhatsApp: typed text, voice notes, and any CV or document you upload. Your contact details. Records of what you paid for.

Why

To generate the CV or cover letter you asked for. Nothing else. CareerSignal doesn't sell your information or use it to build a profile of you for any other purpose.

Who processes it

CareerSignal runs on WhatsApp (operated by Meta), stores your information with Supabase, and uses Anthropic's Claude to read and write CV text. These are real service providers CareerSignal depends on to work at all, not information shared for marketing or resale. Exact data hosting regions are to be confirmed.

How long it's kept

Payment records are kept for 7 years, as required by the Companies Act, 2008. Everything else, your career information, documents, and conversation history, can be deleted or exported by you at any time, directly in WhatsApp, from wherever you are in a conversation. Deleting your data removes it everywhere except those payment records.

Consent

Before your information is used to generate anything, CareerSignal is built to ask you to confirm you're comfortable with how it'll be used, not to bury that in a separate terms document you never see.
