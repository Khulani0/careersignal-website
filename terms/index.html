Terms of Service

The terms that apply when you use CareerSignal to build a CV or career document.

What you're paying for

CareerSignal generates a CV, and at the Advanced tier a cover letter, based on the information you provide over WhatsApp: typed text, a voice note, an uploaded document, or a combination. Basic is R30 for a one-page CV, Mid is R50 for a two-page CV, and Advanced is R90 for up to a three-page CV plus a cover letter tailored to a job you specify.

Preview before payment

You see your CV as plain text before you're asked to pay. Payment happens through a WhatsApp payment link after you approve that preview, not before.

Revisions

Basic includes one AI revision round, Mid includes two. You describe what to change in plain language and CareerSignal edits the relevant section, not the whole document. Advanced includes unlimited AI revisions plus one human review pass.

What you provide

You're responsible for the accuracy of the information you give CareerSignal about your own work history, education, and skills. CareerSignal doesn't independently verify what you tell it, and it doesn't add anything you didn't say.

Acceptable use

CareerSignal is for building your own CV or a CV for someone you're directly helping with their permission. Don't use it to fabricate someone else's credentials or misrepresent who a document is for.

Changes

These terms may be updated as the product changes. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date once that process is in place.
